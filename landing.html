How it works

A simple workflow: describe the situation, classify the crisis, generate a plan, and track daily progress.

1
Share the crisis User writes in natural language or uploads a related document image.
2
Analyze needs The system detects crisis type, urgency, and risk flags.
3
Score recovery Life Restart Score gives a structured starting point.
4
Create a plan 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day recovery guidance.
5
Track progress Daily Coach and Progress Tracker support momentum.
